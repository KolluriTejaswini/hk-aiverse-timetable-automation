## Limitations and unresolved items

- Detailed course syllabi and the applicable academic regulations were not available for complete verification at the time of writing.
- Job-posting URLs and role requirements still need direct verification. Relative publication dates from an earlier search are not treated as current dates.
- The handbook's printed Semester III credit total differs from the sum of its listed course credits. The meaning of the `#` audit-course notation and the treatment of the separately listed summer internship also require confirmation from the institution.
- No faculty or graduate feedback is claimed unless an actual response is documented in `feedback/`.
- This is a student proposal, not an official curriculum revision. Any proposed changes require review and approval by the institution.

## Responsible use

AI assistance was used to help draft and organise material. Verification scripts provide evidence for the specific tests they run; they do not establish that every curriculum statement, external source, or recommendation is correct. Unverified information and assumptions are identified rather than presented as confirmed facts.
