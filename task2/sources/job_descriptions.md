## Requirements observed in the accessible listings

### JD-A — Amgen, Senior Machine Learning Engineer

The accessible regional listing describes work involving ML applications and platforms, data pipelines, model training and evaluation, deployment, experiment tracking, monitoring, and MLOps practices. It mentions Python, version control, test-driven development, cloud technologies, and hands-on AI/ML model training and serving.

The accessible listing states a requirement of 4–8 years of experience. This is a senior/experienced role, not a typical fresher position.

### JD-B — Amgen, Sr Machine Learning Engineer, AI Studio

The listing describes end-to-end AI product development, Python and SQL, APIs, statistics, machine learning, deep learning, NLP, generative AI, RAG, agentic systems, evaluation, security, and MLOps/LLMOps.

The listing states 8–13 years of experience. It is a senior-level reference and should not be presented as an entry-level requirement.

## Limitations

- Only the accessible listing details are used as evidence for the requirements above.
- JD-C and JD-D remain unverified; they must not be treated as confirmed evidence for a specific skill.
- The accessible Amgen listings are experienced or senior roles. Their full skill descriptions are useful as long-term industry references, but their experience requirements are not suitable as direct targets for a new graduate.
- Job descriptions change or disappear. The links should be checked again before using this comparison for a hiring decision.
- This comparison supports a student curriculum proposal; it does not establish that a particular course is officially required by an employer.
